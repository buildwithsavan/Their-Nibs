Refund Policy

At Their Nibs, customer happiness is at the heart of everything we do. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are pleased to offer a straightforward refund process.

28-Day Returns Window

You have 28 calendar days from the date of delivery to return your unworn, unwashed pyjamas or sleepwear accessories for a full refund back to your original payment method.

Item Condition Requirements

To qualify for a refund, returned items must:

  • Be brand new, unworn, unwashed, and odour-free.
  • Have all original Their Nibs swing tags and labels firmly attached.
  • Be folded inside the original protective tissue packaging or presentation box.

Refund Processing Timeline

Once your returned parcel arrives back at our London warehouse:

  • Our quality inspection team will process the return within 2 to 4 working days.
  • You will receive an automated confirmation email as soon as the refund is issued.
  • Refunds typically appear on your bank or card statement within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card issuer or payment gateway.

Damaged or Faulty Items

Every piece is thoroughly inspected before shipping, but if you believe you have received a faulty or damaged item, please email us right away at info@their-nibs.com with photographs of the fault. We will arrange an immediate replacement or full refund, including return postage.

Sale & Promotional Items

Items purchased during promotional events or archive sales are eligible for return within 14 days, unless explicitly designated as final clearance on the product page.

Need Help With a Return?

Our customer support team is always happy to assist with any questions. Please reach out via info@their-nibs.com or message us on our Contact Us page.

Welcome to Their Nibs

Founded in Notting Hill, London in 2003, Their Nibs began with a simple passion: to reinvent nightwear into something expressive, comfortable, and full of vintage character. What started as an intimate West London boutique has blossomed into a beloved British lifestyle brand, celebrated across the country for our bespoke, hand-drawn prints and thoughtful tailoring.

As an independent, female-founded brand, we design every single piece with care. We know that pyjamas are the clothes you wear for your most relaxed moments—slow Sunday mornings with coffee, cosy evenings with loved ones, and peaceful nights of sleep. That’s why we obsess over every fabric touch, contrast piping detail, and button finish.

Hand-Drawn In London

Every print begins as an original illustration or watercolour in our London design studio, making our patterns truly unique.

Cloud-Soft Fabrics

From velvety brushed cotton flannel to fluid liquid satin and breathable voile, we select only the softest fabrics that wash beautifully.

Conscious Craft

We work with ethical partner factories, utilise sustainable cotton blends, and package our orders in recyclable presentation wrap.

Giving Back: 20% to Children's Charity

Giving back has always been at the heart of Their Nibs. We proudly donate 20% of all profits from our Kids & Tweens collection to our chosen children’s charity, helping support vulnerable young people and families across the UK.

Our Boutiques & Community

In 2026, we opened our brand new flagship store in Kensal Rise, London (103 Chamberlayne Road), creating a warm, vibrant space where you can touch our fabrics and chat with our team. You can also find our collections in 14 John Lewis department stores and select independent boutiques nationwide.

Easy 28-Day Returns Policy

If your pyjamas are not quite the right size, shape, or colour, you have 28 days from the date of delivery to return them to us.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original pristine condition.
  • All original swing tags and packaging must remain attached.
  • For hygiene reasons, scrunchies, shower caps, and earrings cannot be returned if opened, unless faulty.

How to Return Your Item

  1. Package your unworn items securely in the original bag or suitable protective mailer.
  2. Include your order packing slip or order number and contact details inside the parcel.
  3. Email our team on info@their-nibs.com with your order number to request a pre-paid Royal Mail return label.
  4. Drop your parcel off at any local Post Office branch or Royal Mail Parcel Postbox.

Exchanges

Need a different size? We offer free size exchanges on UK orders, subject to stock availability. Simply note the requested replacement size on your returns note.

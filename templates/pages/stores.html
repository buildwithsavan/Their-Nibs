Find Your Nibs: Come and See Us in Store

There’s something lovely about discovering your next favourite pair of pyjamas in person. Feeling the fabric, taking a closer look at the print and finding the colours that make you smile. While our online shop is always there for a browse from the sofa, you can also find Their Nibs out in the world, including at our very own shop in Kensal Rise.

Our Flagship Store

Our own little corner of Kensal Rise

We’ve opened the doors to our new store at 103 Chamberlayne Road, Kensal Rise, London, NW10 3NS, right opposite Kensal Rise station.

It’s a little piece of the Their Nibs world, where you can explore our prints up close, feel our fabrics and get a helping hand choosing something lovely. Whether you’re treating yourself, searching for a thoughtful gift or simply passing through the neighbourhood, we’d love you to pop in.

As an independent, female-founded brand, having a space where we can meet our customers means so much to us. We’re looking forward to familiar faces, new neighbours and plenty of conversations about pyjamas.

Opening Times

  • Monday & Tuesday Closed
  • Wednesday to Saturday 10am – 6pm
  • Sunday 12noon – 4pm

Find us in 14 John Lewis stores

Further afield, you can discover Their Nibs in 14 John Lewis stores across the United Kingdom, making it easier to browse a selection of our signature nightwear in person.

  • John Lewis Oxford Street, London
  • Peter Jones, Sloane Square, London
  • John Lewis Edinburgh, St James Quarter
  • John Lewis Trafford Centre, Manchester
  • John Lewis Leeds, Victoria Gate
  • Plus 9 further boutique John Lewis departments nationwide

Discover us at independent boutiques

You’ll also find Their Nibs in a selection of independent boutiques. We love being part of these shops, where thoughtfully chosen collections and personal service make finding something special a pleasure.

Shopping with our independent stockists supports both their businesses and ours and is a lovely way to keep independent retail thriving.

Interested in stocking Their Nibs? Contact our wholesale team at wholesale@their-nibs.com.

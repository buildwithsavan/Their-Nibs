How to Measure Yourself

1. Bust / Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your bust or chest, keeping the measuring tape comfortably level under your arms.

2. Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, where your trousers naturally sit. Do not pull the tape too tight.

3. Hips

Stand with your feet together and measure around the widest part of your hips and bottom.

4. Inside Leg

Measure from the top of your inner thigh down to the bottom of your ankle bone.
